TRINETRA AI: CRITICAL RESEARCH SYNTHESIS & VALIDATION SUMMARY
======================================================================================

1. MOST DEFENSIBLE POSITIONING:
   -> POSITIONING 2: Novel Engineering Product / Systems Architecture.
   -> Justification: The 244-paper dataset proves that individual component methods 
      (fraud classifiers, blockchain provenance, ODR systems, XAI models) exist in 
      isolation, but no commercial or academic system orchestrates them into a unified, 
      cross-organizational evidence reconciliation platform.

2. CORE PAPERS VALIDATION SUMMARY:
   -> Core papers addressing multi-org reconciliation: PARTIALLY (20% Truly Core, 80% Component).
   -> Key Insight: The core papers provide essential algorithmic building blocks (e.g. ESPRES 
      smart contracts [TRINETRA_0001], eBay dispute prediction [TRINETRA_0197], apparel 
      return taxonomy [TRINETRA_0502]), validating the necessity of TriNetra's integrative engine.

3. MAJOR CHALLENGES IDENTIFIED IN LITERATURE:
   a. Privacy & Commercial Barriers: Cross-org data sharing is restricted by GDPR and commercial secrecy (TRINETRA_0550).
   b. Customer Friction vs Fraud Rates: Fraud affects 5-11% of fashion returns (TRINETRA_0502); heavy verification must not alienate honest buyers.
   c. Data Asymmetry: Subjective text claims fail to resolve disputes without objective sensor/weight/video evidence (TRINETRA_0197).

4. WHAT TRINETRA MUST DO BEFORE ARCHITECTURE PHASE (ACTION ITEMS):
   [1] Implement Privacy-Preserving Hashes: Use SHA-256 evidence fingerprints and zero-knowledge proof tokens instead of sharing raw PII across stakeholders.
   [2] Enforce Adaptive Inspection Friction: Pair Trust Intelligence with risk scoring so that 90%+ of low-risk returns undergo friction-free automated processing.
   [3] Standardize Multi-Modal Evidence Schemas: Define unified JSON schemas for carrier weight logs, packaging scans, unboxing video hashes, and serial numbers.
